## What happens to a row you rule on

- **"My book answers this"** → the fact keeps its answer and gains a `source` citation. Several
  of these have no `source` field at all, which is part of why they were flagged.
- **"My book does not answer this"** → the fact is **retired, not deleted** (R-4): it keeps its id
  and its place in `DECK`, drops out of `ACTIVE`, and carries the sentence saying why. That is
  what happened to the sixteen in August and to the ceremony trio on 10 August.
- **"I am not sure"** → it stays live and stays on this list. An unanswerable fact drilled to
  permanence is worse than one you never met, but so is deleting a correct one.
